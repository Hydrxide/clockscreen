The timezone and the location are separate: the clock (and the hours on the forecast) keep the timezone's time even when the weather is for somewhere else. Changes apply straight away. They're saved by clockscreen and win over config.yaml (which HController's dashboard edits) until you press Reset to config. Narrow a place down with a region or country after a comma: Portland, OR, London, Ontario. US ZIP codes work; Canadian postal codes don't, so use the city or latitude, longitude.
